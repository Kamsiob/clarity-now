A

THE MARK

The gradient square and the check are gone. A check mark says "to do list," which is the category the app is arguing against, and a gradient is on the tell list. The new mark is the app's actual idea: one thing at the front, the rest waiting behind it. Flat background, no gradient, two values of one color. It reads as a stack of cards seen face on, and at 24px it still reads as "one solid thing with things behind it."
App icon
Flat #141A2E, mark in #F3F1EC.
No gradient. Two colors total.
Holds down to 22px. The two waiting cards merge into a single suggestion of depth rather than disappearing.

Monochrome and inverse
For the notification icon, the widget header, the settings row and any single color context. The mark survives being reduced to one value because the depth is carried by opacity, not by hue.
status bar, widget, notification

What it is not

✕ a check mark, which says to do list
✕ a gradient square, on the 2026 tell list
✕ an orb with rings, which is Kam AI
✕ a letter C, which is the obvious answer
✕ three equal bars, which reads as a menu

Why the proportions matter. The front card is 72 wide and 40 tall. The two behind are 56 and 40 wide and only 12 and 11 tall, so they read as edges rather than as cards in their own right. Equal sizes would read as a list. Unequal sizes with the largest in front reads as depth.

the mark has to survive being obvious in hindsight
B

SWIPE GESTURES

Three actions, two directions, and delete is never a full swipe. Right reveals Complete and a full swipe commits it. Left reveals Swap and Delete side by side; a full swipe left commits Swap, the safe one. Delete requires an explicit tap on the revealed button and then goes through the five second undo. Destructive actions should never be reachable by momentum.
